Cookie policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Català · Castellano

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website may store on your device when you visit it.

2. Cookies we use

This website does not use cookies, neither our own nor third-party. It only stores two technical items in your browser, needed for the guide to work as you ask: (1) the language you choose, in your browser’s local storage (“pb_lang”), and (2) a copy of the guide’s files (browser cache) so you can use it without a signal. They contain no personal data, do not track you and are not shared with anyone, and they do not require consent. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings. That is why we do not show a cookie banner.

3. Third-party websites

If you go from here to third-party websites (Instagram, WhatsApp, Lets Holidays, etc.), they may use their own cookies. Please check their respective policies.

The live cameras (ipcamlive.com, YouTube, Windy) load only when you press their button, and those services may use their own cookies.

4. Changes

If we introduce cookies in the future that are not strictly necessary, we will tell you and ask for your consent before using them.

5. Contact

For any question: info@fibrapages.es. See also the Legal notice and the Privacy policy.
